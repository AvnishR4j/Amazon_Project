Problem Statement

The Challenge

Amazon processes millions of deliveries daily. Predicting which deliveries are at risk of delay enables proactive intervention and improved customer satisfaction.

Our Objective

Build a multi-class classification model to predict delivery delay risk:

  • On-Time - Delivery within 90 minutes
  • At-Risk - Delivery between 91-160 minutes
  • Delayed - Delivery exceeding 160 minutes

Haversine Distance Formula

d = 2r × arcsin(√(sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat₁)×cos(lat₂)×sin²(Δlon/2)))
